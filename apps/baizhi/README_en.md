## Introduction

Baizhi Office (MonkeyAI) is an open-source team AI workspace. Describe a task in natural language and let AI deliver results across office work, coding and design, with shared resources, centralized management and self-hosting.

## Features

- **Office work**: Create presentations, write documents, process spreadsheets and organize files.
- **Coding**: Assist with architecture design, code generation and bug fixing.
- **Design**: Create posters, graphic designs, interactive designs and short-form productions.
- **Shared resources**: Share models, knowledge bases, skills, experts, MCP connectors and rules; connect your own models and business tools.
- **Team management**: Manage resource usage and billing, access permissions, audit records and team statistics.
- **Self-hosting**: Operate the server on your own infrastructure and control team data, backups and extensions.

The free edition supports up to 10 team seats. Cloud computers, technical support and other paid benefits are subject to the official website.
